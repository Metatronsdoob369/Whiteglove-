WhiteGlove circadian maintenance — WAKE and DREAM cycles.

The vault maintains itself on a heartbeat. The WAKE cycle scans the intake
paths for new knowledge and shatters fresh documents into shards. The DREAM
cycle re-indexes the vault and verifies integrity: every stored fingerprint
is recomputed and compared, and drift beyond the shard-drift threshold is
flagged rather than silently accepted.

All vectors pass an L2 normalization gate at ingestion, enforced by the
embedding contract, so every shard lives on the unit sphere and distance
comparisons stay honest. A least-frequently-used cache keeps hot shards in
memory, and a hot ring buffer pins the most critical landmarks for
constant-time access.

The design goal is a portable archive drive that stays trustworthy offline:
no network dependency, no cloud calls, and a re-index that can prove to
itself that nothing rotted while the machine slept.
